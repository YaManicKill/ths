## Privacy

The Harvest Season website does not collect, store, or share any personal data about
its visitors. There are no accounts, no analytics profiles, and no advertising
trackers.

The show's own publishing tools use Google's YouTube API solely to upload The Harvest
Season's episodes to The Harvest Season's own YouTube channel. They access no data
about anyone other than the show itself, and store nothing beyond the credentials
Google issues to the show for that purpose.

If you contact us through the [contact form](/contact/), we receive what you send and
use it only to reply.
